# Envision the Heavens™ — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026 (the app's name changed; the policy did not)

Envision the Heavens™ ("Envision" or "the app"), formerly ExploreStars Envision, is a telescope-mount controller for Explore Scientific™ PMC-Eight™ mounts, developed by Wes McDonald, an OpenGOTO™ Community developer. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## The short version

- The app does **not** collect, store, or share personal information with the developer or anyone else.
- There are **no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking**.
- Everything the app needs stays on your device, except object-name lookups you choose to make on Wikipedia (see below).

## Information the app uses

### Location
With your permission, the app uses your device's location to set your observing site (latitude and longitude) for the sky chart and mount alignment. Your location is saved **only on your device** and is never sent to the developer or any third party. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings and enter your site manually instead.

### Compass and motion sensors
The app reads your device's compass and motion sensors to show where your phone is pointing in the sky. This data is used in real time on your device and is **not stored or transmitted**.

### Local network
The app connects to your PMC-Eight mount over your local Wi-Fi network. Commands and status information travel **only between your device and your mount**; nothing is sent over the internet.

Your PMC-Eight mount may store Wi-Fi network settings you enter into it (such as your network name and password). These are kept on the mount and are never accessible to the developer. If you sell or lend your mount, clear its Wi-Fi settings first.

### Wikipedia and Wikidata lookups
When you open information about a celestial object, the app requests that object's article from Wikipedia and Wikidata, operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. That request includes the object's name and your chosen language, along with the standard technical information any web request carries (such as your IP address). The Wikimedia Foundation's [Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) applies to those requests. The developer does not receive this information.

### Settings
Your preferences (mount model, connection settings, observing site, display options) are stored **only on your device** and are deleted when you delete the app.

### External links
Some links in the app (for example, to Explore Scientific's website) open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
